One fabric, four views

A shared standard for nursing knowledge

Every lane uses the same package, provenance, review, licensing, versioning, and retirement model. The lanes organize discovery; they do not create competing sources of truth.

Learn

Learning libraries

Study guides, concept maps, quizzes, illustrations, and multilingual explainers—without claiming grading, competency, or faculty authority.

Practice

Professional libraries

Evidence-reading packs, generic specialty references, and reflective tools—not local policy or patient-specific guidance.

Lead

Leadership libraries

Huddle tools, educator guides, implementation lessons, and professional wisdom—bounded from institutional authorization.

Build

Creator libraries

Browser-local dashboards, designs, templates, and synthetic simulations—without implying technical, clinical, or educational approval.

Governance spine

Portable knowledge. Explicit authority.

Content packages are the source of truth. Search, vector, and graph indexes are disposable derivatives.
Contributors propose. Reviewers verify. Institutions authorize. Nurses steward.
Inclusion means governed availability—not endorsement, certification, clinical validation, or permission to change practice.

Honest status

What exists—and what does not

This first baseline establishes a repository and public documentation surface. It does not launder planned capabilities into present-tense claims.

Present now

  • Doctrine and operational playbook
  • Public governance and safety boundaries
  • Empty portable catalog projections
  • Static, browser-readable holding page
  • Deterministic repository verification

Not active

  • Published or accepted Knowledge Packs
  • Reviewer council or institutional authority
  • Hosted RAG, vector search, or graph traversal
  • Accounts, payments, marketplace, or school tenants
  • PHI, clinical decision, competency, or autonomous action
