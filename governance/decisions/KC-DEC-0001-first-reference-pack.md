# KC-DEC-0001: First reference Knowledge Pack

## Decision

Approve **AI Literacy Foundations for Nurses** as the first English, D0/Green, Learn-lane reference Knowledge Pack for the NIN Knowledge Commons.

The English edition must be stabilized and reviewed before any Tagalog edition is created.

## Approved scope

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Title | AI Literacy Foundations for Nurses |
| Language | English |
| Data class | D0 |
| EDENA risk tier | Green |
| Library lane | Learn |
| Role | First reference Knowledge Pack |
| Localization sequence | Stabilize and review the English edition before creating a Tagalog edition |

A working Pack identifier may be proposed during schema and namespace design. It is not adopted by this decision.

## Purpose

The Pack will provide foundational, non-clinical AI literacy for nurses. It is intended to establish and test the Commons' source, rights, provenance, review, integrity, lifecycle, chunking, catalog, citation, and retrieval contracts before broader seeding.

## Authority boundary

This decision authorizes Pack scoping and English-edition development. It does **not**:

- adopt a canonical Pack, catalog, namespace, review, chunk, graph, or localization schema;
- accept or publish candidate Pack bytes;
- establish independent review, certification, accreditation, clinical validation, or institutional authorization;
- authorize clinical decision support, patient-specific guidance, competency determination, or changes to practice;
- create a Tagalog edition before the English source is stable and reviewed;
- activate contributor intake, a reviewer council, hosted search, vector retrieval, graph traversal, RAG, marketplace functions, R2 distribution, or `packs.nurse-ai-os.org`;
- permit PHI, private contributor records, confidential institutional material, restricted curricula, credentials, secrets, or unlicensed third-party content.

Repository inclusion or future publication will mean availability for governed use—not endorsement, certification, clinical validation, institutional authorization, or permission to change practice.

## Required English-edition gates

Before publication is proposed, the English edition must have:

1. an adopted Pack and supporting schema set with adversarial fixtures and validator tests;
2. a bounded learning scope, audience, intended uses, prohibited uses, limitations, and correction route;
3. documented authorship, sources, provenance, licenses, third-party rights, and AI-assistance disclosure;
4. D0/no-PHI verification and Green-tier classification review;
5. deterministic content-unit identities, inventory, checksums, catalog projection, and rebuildable index projections;
6. accessibility and plain-language review;
7. independent human review appropriate to the educational content;
8. review and publication decisions bound to the exact candidate version and digest;
9. explicit founder judgment at the publication gate;
10. post-publication distribution, citation, correction, withdrawal, and recall verification.

## Tagalog hold

A Tagalog edition remains held until an exact English source version and digest are stable and reviewed. Any later edition must:

- identify that exact source version and digest;
- declare direct translation versus cultural adaptation;
- confirm translation and adaptation rights;
- disclose machine or AI assistance;
- receive human language and cultural review;
- receive subject review where meaning could affect educational or safety boundaries;
- maintain its own version, review state, limitations, and stale-source warning.
